Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 November 2026**

Introduction

Red Island Media is an independent media organisation dedicated to economic issues, public policy and development, with a particular focus on Madagascar. The purpose of this privacy policy is to explain how the personal data of visitors to the website (https://redisland.media) is processed and to set out the rights available to users. Red Island Media is committed to complying with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the French Data Protection Act and all applicable provisions relating to the protection of personal data.

Data Controller

The data controller for personal data collected directly by Red Island Media is: - Red Island Media. - As soon as a data controller is appointed is name will be communicated in this privacy policy page. - The official contact adress is contact@redisland.media For any queries regarding this policy or the protection of personal data, visitors may contact Red Island Media at the address given above.

Personal data collected

As at the date of this policy’s update, Red Island Media provide an online contact adress, a newsletter subscription and a voluntarily option for collecting personal data. In particular: - An email addresses are currently collected from visitors that chose to receive our newsletter; - A newsletter subscription is offered; - An advertising mechanisms specific to Red Island Media are currently active and optional when cookies are removed. Visiting the website involves the use of its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, which may process certain technical data relating to visitors.

Hosting and technical data

The Red Island Media website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. According to GitHub’s official documentation, the IP addresses of visitors to websites hosted on GitHub Pages are recorded and retained for security purposes, regardless of whether or not visitors have a GitHub account. This technical processing is carried out by GitHub for its own purposes. Red Island Media does not implement any mechanisms designed to collect or use these IP addresses. For further information on the processing carried out by GitHub, visitors may consult its privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement/

Purposes and legal bases

To date, Red Island Media does not carry out any processing involving the deliberate collection of personal data from its visitors. Any technical processing carried out by service providers involved in hosting the website serves their own purposes, in particular the security and operation of their services. Should Red Island Media implement new personal data processing activities, their purposes and legal bases will be specified in an update to this policy.

Recipients of data

Red Island Media does currently pass on personal data collected directly from optional cookies validated by its visitors to commercial or advertising partners. GitHub may process the technical data necessary for the provision and security of its service, in accordance with its own terms and conditions and privacy policy. Any new service provider involved in the processing of personal data on behalf of Red Island Media will be identified in an update to this policy where necessary.

Retention periods

Red Island Media does not currently maintain any database containing personal information collected voluntarily from visitors. The retention periods for any technical data processed by GitHub are governed by the retention policy defined by that service provider. Should new processing operations be introduced, the corresponding retention periods will be specified in this policy.

Users’ rights

In accordance with the GDPR, any data subject whose personal data is being processed has the following rights, subject to the conditions and limitations set out in the regulations: the right of access; the right to rectification; the right to erasure; the right to restriction of processing; the right to object; the right to data portability, where applicable. To exercise their rights in relation to processing for which Red Island Media is responsible, visitors may submit their request to: contact@redisland.media Requests relating to processing carried out directly by GitHub must be addressed to GitHub in accordance with the procedures set out in its privacy policy. Anyone who believes that their rights have not been respected may also lodge a complaint with the French Data Protection Authority (CNIL): https://www.cnil.fr

Data security

Red Island Media takes care to limit the personal data collected and to implement measures appropriate to the nature of the processing it carries out. Technical service providers involved in the operation of the website apply their own security measures in accordance with their respective obligations.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

This policy may be amended to take account of changes to the website, its features and the applicable regulations. In particular, the introduction of a contact form, a newsletter, audience measurement tools or advertising features may require this policy to be updated before they are activated. The most recent version will always be available on the Red Island Media website.
