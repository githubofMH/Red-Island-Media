Personal data collected As at the date of this policy’s update, Red Island Media does not provide any contact forms, newsletter subscription forms or other means of voluntarily collecting personal data. According to GitHub’s official documentation, the IP addresses of visitors to websites hosted on GitHub Pages are recorded and retained for security purposes, regardless of whether or not visitors have a GitHub account. This technical processing is carried out by GitHub for its own purposes. Red Island Media does not implement any mechanisms designed to collect or use these IP addresses. Should Red Island Media implement new personal data processing activities, their purposes and legal bases will be specified in an update to this policy. ## 6. Recipients of data Red Island Media does not currently pass on any personal data collected directly from its visitors to commercial or advertising partners. GitHub may process the technical data necessary for the provision and security of its service, in accordance with its own terms and conditions and privacy policy. Any new service provider involved in the processing of personal data on behalf of Red Island Media will be identified in an update to this policy where necessary. ## 7.
