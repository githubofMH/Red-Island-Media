About Red Island Media

Red Island Media is an independent media organisation that publishes articles based on major newspaper headlines relating to economic issues. Red Island Media focuses on topics concerning Madagascar and does not cover foreign news unless it has a subsequent impact on the country’s economic situation. While maintaining high standards in its publications, Red Island Media does not claim to be an academic media organisation. We report on topics in greater depth than traditional media outlets, employing qualitative and quantitative methods. At Red Island Media, we are committed to transparency, which we believe to be the most important element in maintaining a critical stance. To this end, we collaborate with journalists and major media organisations to provide a more nuanced and in-depth perspective on a particular subject. We do not align ourselves with any particular school of thought or economic philosophy. Our mission is to analyse information in the spirit of explanatory journalism, acting as a mediator of information and its interpretation.

Our mission

Our mission is to foster critical thinking in local economic debates. We aim to provide everyone with information, supplemented by modern tools, to challenge overly hasty ways of thinking on economic issues that shape our economic circumstances. The website is designed to be educational whilst upholding scientific rigour.
